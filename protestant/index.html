The Living System of Protestant Formation

Congregational discipleship across denominations

This model frames Protestant spiritual formation as participation in a self-producing congregational system: word and sacrament, ministries and a shared church year. Members choose a church rather than a parish territory. Choose a denomination preset to tune worship, baptism and ministry patterns, then run the congregation forward in time.

    Description

    Choose an event (revival week, guest preacher, mission trip return, leadership conference or VBS) and tap the sphere. Enthusiasm ripples through members, who glow gold. While it lasts they worship more often, occasional attenders return, and seekers arrive. What remains depends on ministries that can absorb it. Six months later, the log reports how much stayed.

    Use the denomination preset to compare non-denominational evangelical, Southern Baptist, Assemblies of God and PCA calibration. A denominational landscape view across Protestant bodies is planned; this release is congregation-scale only.

    Data and sources

    Each rule is tagged: calibrated, partly grounded, or hypothesis. Presets differ on baptism rates, weekly worship and Communion frequency.

    • Weekly worship among U.S. evangelicals calibrated Pew Religious Landscape Study
    • Denomination presets (non-denom, SBC, AG, PCA): infant and adult baptism, leaving and switching partly grounded Pew and denominational reports
    • Ministry participation beyond Sunday partly grounded Pew congregational life surveys
    • Parent and childhood formation partly grounded Smith and Adamczyk, Handing Down the Faith
    • Pandemic attendance dip and recovery partly grounded national congregation studies
    • Protestant events, worship intensity, closed leadership, ministry renewal rates hypothesis
    • Denominational landscape macro planned
