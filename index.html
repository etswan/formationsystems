    How to read it

    Click near members to bring them a hardship: a bereavement, a job loss, an illness. Their bonds drop, and whether they recover depends on the relations around them: their groups, Sunday Mass, ministry to the sick and reconciliation. Six months later, the log reports how they fared. Try the same click under closed leadership and compare.

    Attendance is not fixed. Each year some regular attenders slip into occasional practice and some occasional attenders return, often at a marriage, a child’s baptism or a great feast. Formation in childhood, a parent’s practice, spiritual direction, the young adults group and, as a hypothesis, the reverence of the liturgy all shift those chances.

    Leave it running for decades and the parish remakes itself: people are born, age, join groups, leave, die and are replaced by births, converts and people moving in. The members change while the organisation persists: autopoiesis.

    Watch the age pyramid and the regeneration ratio. A parish can hold its numbers for years while ageing towards a point where it can no longer replace itself.

    Data and sources

    Each rule is tagged by how well it is grounded: calibrated to published data, partly grounded, or a hypothesis.

    • Weekly Mass attendance: 28% of cradle Catholics, 38% of converts; Communion at every Mass: 34% and 58% calibrated Pew 2025
    • Starting age structure of adult members calibrated Pew Religious Landscape Study 2023–24; share of children assumed at about one in five partly grounded
    • Infant baptisms about 7.4 per 1,000 Catholics a year calibrated Vatican yearbook via NCR
    • Adults received about 1.3 per 1,000 Catholics a year (2023 U.S. adult baptisms and receptions), +38% in the 2026 setting partly grounded Official Catholic Directory via Catholic Register, Hallow 2026
    • Pathways in: relational (family, friends, marriage) and content or experience (liturgy, digital); two-thirds cite digital content partly grounded 2026 OCIA survey
    • Leaving: tuned so that about 1.1% of members leave a year, consistent with Pew's 8.4 leavers per convert partly grounded Pew 2025
    • Deaths by age: approximate U.S. life-table rates partly grounded; moving in and out at about 1% a year each assumption
    • Pandemic: attendance falls to about 60% of normal for about three years, then recovers calibrated CARA via Aleteia
    • Scandal: raises leaving for about two years; 39% of former Catholics cite scandal as an important reason they left partly grounded Pew 2025
    • Adult practice is shaped by parents’ practice and childhood formation partly grounded Smith and Adamczyk, Handing Down the Faith (Oxford, 2021)
    • Returns to practice at marriage, a child’s baptism and great feasts; more slipping during scandal and pandemic partly grounded in the direction of the effect; the rates are assumptions
    • Closed leadership, the dropped calendar, the effects of spiritual direction and of liturgical reverence on practice, the hardship response, and all group renewal rates hypothesis
    • Whole-Church figures: Catholics, priests and growth by continent for 2024 calibrated Fides 2026, Annuarium 2024; priests in Africa, Europe and Oceania derived from reported ratios partly grounded
